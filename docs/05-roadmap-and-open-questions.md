# 5. Roadmap, risks and open questions

## Phased roadmap

**Phase 0: Validate (2–4 weeks, now)**
- Use this prototype in 15–20 conversations with restaurant owners. Ask: what do you pay apps today? Would you
  put a QR in every bag? Would you pay ₹1,499/month? Who would take orders on your side?
- Talk to 30+ regular customers: would you scan a QR to save ₹100–200? What would make you trust it?

**Phase 1: Concierge pilot (6–8 weeks)**
- 5–10 restaurants in one area. Real storefronts, UPI payments, WhatsApp order alerts, **pickup + own riders only**.
- Do things manually where possible (we set up menus, we call restaurants on missed orders).

**Phase 2: v1 product (2–3 months)**
- Backend, restaurant PWA, card payments with split settlement, one delivery partner integration,
  loyalty, customer list, WhatsApp offers. Subscription billing.

**Phase 3: Density & growth**
- 50+ restaurants per neighbourhood → directory becomes a discovery channel.
- ONDC listing, POS integrations, multi-outlet, custom domains, AI tools.
- Expand neighbourhood by neighbourhood, city by city.

## Ideas worth exploring

- **"Order direct" bag stickers on aggregator orders**: the cheapest acquisition channel we'll ever have.
- **Group / office ordering**: one link, everyone adds their items, single delivery.
- **Pre-order & scheduled orders**: lunch tiffins, party orders, festival sweets.
- **Subscriptions from restaurants**: weekly meal plans, monthly tiffin.
- **Table QR for dine-in**: order and pay at table using the same storefront.
- **Neighbourhood bundles**: share one rider across nearby restaurants for lower delivery cost.
- **Restaurant co-op angle**: restaurants associations (e.g. NRAI members) as partners/distribution.
- **AI everything for setup**: menu from photo, photo enhancement, auto-written story, offer suggestions
  ("Tuesday is slow, send 10% off to lapsed customers?").
- **Transparent receipts**: every receipt shows "You saved ₹X by ordering direct". Customers share that.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Customers don't change habits; apps are convenient | Focus on repeat customers of each restaurant; make savings visible; loyalty only available direct |
| Restaurants fear upsetting the aggregators | Position as complementary; we don't need exclusivity |
| Delivery is expensive/unreliable without a fleet | Start with pickup + own riders; partner logistics at cost; neighbourhood batching |
| Missed or slow order acceptance | WhatsApp + sound alerts + IVR call + auto-refund |
| Small restaurants won't pay subscription | Free tier; or small fixed per-order fee as an alternative |
| Competitors (existing zero-commission tools, POS players adding ordering) | Win on simplicity, field onboarding, "looks like your place" design, and density in chosen areas |
| Regulatory (GST e-commerce operator rules, payments) | Get CA/legal advice early; never hold funds |

## Open questions for the team

1. **Name**: is "DirectDish" good? Alternatives: SeedhaOrder, ApnaMenu, ZeroCut, Thali Direct, OrderSeedha.
2. **Pricing**: flat subscription vs tiny fixed per-order fee? What's the right free tier limit?
3. **First city & neighbourhood**: where do we have contacts with restaurant owners?
4. **Delivery**: pickup-only pilot first, or integrate a logistics partner from day one?
5. **Customer app or no app?** Storefronts are websites (no install). Do we ever need a customer app?
6. **How much do we lean on the directory** vs purely powering restaurants' own sites?
7. **Competition research**: how do Thrive, DotPe, Petpooja online ordering, and ONDC buyer apps price and
   perform? Why did similar efforts struggle? What would we do differently?
8. **Sales model**: field sales in-house, or partner with POS resellers / restaurant consultants?
